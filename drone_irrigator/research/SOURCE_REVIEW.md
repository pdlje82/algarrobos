## Evidence/adoption boundaries

The checked candidate adds source-backed findings and bounded gaps, links profiles to existing species identities, and displays owner-resolved data in the Explorer. Supplementary web observations retain source URLs, dates and limitations. They are not silently upgraded to registered PDF evidence. Prepared clusters/scenario assumptions remain separately validated, explicitly unadopted native records. Current evidence memberships, live generation rules, prices and save schemas remain unchanged.

Human review here means the agent inspected the stated evidence and report under the user-authorized task. It does not mean the user personally reviewed or approved every passage. Physical measurements, delivered supplier quotes and site permissions remain external work; their absence is visible data.
