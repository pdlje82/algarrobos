## Meaning and limits

The reference layer distinguishes the supplied nursery root ball from a possible mature crown. Neither is the complete root system. Width is not inferred from container volume, plant height, trunk diameter, spacing or the crown of another taxon. Root balls vary with the actual nursery container and production cycle; no exact stock-width measurement was verified for any of the 23 catalogue entries.

The existing library review (LEARN-005, LEARN-007 and LEARN-008; NIJAR-003 pp. 1–2 and 4–6; FOREST-002 pp. 13, 75 and 80; FOREST-003 and FOREST-004) supplies procurement and growth-form context, but no complete table of mature crown and installation root-ball diameters. FOREST-002 p. 13 describes long lateral branches/roots; these are not representative circular diameters for a seedling or this pilot.

External evidence was screened for the exact taxon. Cultivar-only data, height-only descriptions, leaf/fruit dimensions, extreme historical sizes, and sample distributions that mix ages were not converted into typical mature crowns. Unresolved values are deliberate evidence gaps, not omitted validation.

RHS ranges describe ultimate horticultural spread; NCSU describes cultivated landscape width. They are qualified references, not predictions for low-rainfall Almeria or promises of a maturity date. Conflicting contexts are preserved. No suitability or spacing rule is derived from a crown circle.

## How to use this evidence in the designer

- Root-ball outlines appear only after a verified stock measurement or an explicit user assumption; a 1 L label alone produces no circle.
- Range-only mature spreads appear as a band between minimum and maximum diameter, with dashed edges. No midpoint is represented as an average.
- User-entered diameters require an explicit measured/supplied or planning-assumption basis and can include a note. They remain distinct from the reference source.
- Symbols identify tree versus shrub/grass, while actual dimensions are not exaggerated to make them look larger on screen.
- Neither overlapping crowns nor a crown crossing the assumed rectangle is automatically an invalid layout. The old illustrative layout clearance is a separate advanced setting.

## Editable implementation reference

The validated, versioned plant_dimensions.json in the central design_rules library contains the adopted numeric ranges, explicit nulls, stock context, source URLs and limitations. Updating repository evidence requires a new ruleset version and explicit adoption by existing designs.
