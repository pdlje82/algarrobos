## Production and checks

The EPUB 3 archive contains sixteen chapter files, chapter/illustration navigation, compatibility navigation, language metadata, a reflowable stylesheet and seventy-two image resources: twenty original concept diagrams and fifty-two equipment/component/research pictures. Every image is in the reading order at its relevant passage. Text remains text, rather than images of pages. Prose explains diagrams so that the narrative remains understandable without viewing them.

The build checked ZIP integrity, the first uncompressed EPUB mimetype entry, XML parsing, manifest identity and resource presence, chapter order, complete text readback, all seventy-two image references and alt text, packaged-image byte identity, chapter navigation and figure anchors. These are local package checks, not a claimed EPUBCheck certification.

The self-contained browser document was opened in an isolated headless Chrome profile. All seventy-two images loaded, with fifty-two reference-picture captions present. Chapter and image counts were checked, and opening and closing the enlarged-image dialog was exercised. Desktop at 1,280 pixels and mobile at 390 pixels had no horizontal document overflow. Screenshots of diagram/prose passages were inspected at both widths. On a phone, dense technical labels require magnification; the browser copy offers an enlarged view.

The final narration and short picture captions have 7,702 words and an estimated listening duration of 48–59 minutes at 130–160 words per minute. Actual duration depends on voice, pauses and speed. No audio was generated. Direct ElevenReader import, playback, image rendering and word highlighting remain untested. The browser read-along is not playback-synchronised.
