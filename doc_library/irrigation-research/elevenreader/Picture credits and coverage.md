## Rights and preparation

Most manufacturer and project photographs retain their source copyrights; no blanket open redistribution licence is inferred. The Open Irr figures by Andrew M. Bierer are published under CC BY 4.0. The CatWAN repository README states CERN Open Hardware Licence v1.2; the source board identifies Electronic Cats, Montserrat Figueroa and Eduardo Contreras, revision 1.0 dated 15 June 2020.

The original downloads and extracted source images are retained in `photos/originals/`. The PNG reference copies retain the full image frame. Compact EPUB/HTML copies are in `photos/ebook/`: proportionally fitted within 1600 by 1250 pixels, white background for transparency, JPEG quality 88 for most images and PNG for the board layout. No object was generated, retouched, substituted or cropped. Original concept diagrams remain byte-identical. Source and prepared-image hashes are recorded in [the picture manifest](Photo%20manifest.json).

The LIFE NIEBLAS Cocoon photograph is extracted from PDF page 93, and its individual fog/protection-guard photograph from PDF page 95 of the previously inspected 224-page technical manual. [LIFE NIEBLAS / GESPLAN technical manual, legal deposit 2024; website lists Technical Manual 2025 — direct PDF](https://lifenieblas.com/sites/default/files/documentacion/2024/Techcnical%20Manual%20LIFE%20NIEBLAS%20-ENG_0.pdf). The external PDF remains offered for addition, rather than becoming a permanent library holding through temporary image extraction.

The public Dragino product-image host returned HTTP 406; the image used is instead explicitly sourced and labelled from the same manufacturer's current SE01 documentation. PlantCare's app image was replaced during selection by the specifically identified PlantControl CX hardware image. Supplier photographs identify an appearance, not independent evidence of performance or current availability.

The earlier diagram-only EPUB, manuscript and supporting files are preserved in [the edition archive](archive/Diagram-only%20edition%20-%202026-11-03.zip).
