# Irrigation research sources and downloadable documents

Checked 3 November 2026. Companion to [LEARN-016](../LEARN-016%20-%20Almeria%20Restoration%20-%20Irrigation%20Technology%20and%20Demonstrator%20Research%20-%202026-11-03.md).

## Evidence categories and library status

- **Research:** published original studies, with their experimental setting and limits.
- **Technical guidance:** institutional manuals and engineering documentation.
- **Manufacturer:** evidence that a named capability is documented; performance and price claims are not independent comparative findings.
- **Proposal:** original architecture, estimates, diagrams and trial design in LEARN-016. These are not validated site specifications.

New local holdings are the report and its original visual/reproduction companions. The external original PDFs below are **offered for addition**, not permanent `doc_library/` holdings. Several were downloaded into a temporary research cache for text/page inspection. That cache is not the library. The initial twelve concept diagrams are original. Later reference pictures reproduce credited source images; see the listening-edition credits and drone extension.

## Retrieval failures and excluded claims

The research attempted the following leads; unsuccessful access is recorded instead of inventing a working document or treating a snippet as full evidence:

1. **LIFE Green Link final report:** the indexed URL `https://thegreenlink.eu/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/LIFE15-CCAES000125_Final-Report_censored.pdf` redirected to a domain-sales page on checking. It is **not a verified working download**. The separately identified LIFE NIEBLAS manual is used under its own title and provenance, not substituted as the Green Link report.
2. **Almería municipal landscaping PDF, February 2024:** `https://almeriaciudad.es/uploads/media/document/entrada_registro_gmu_01-03-2024_1_anexo1_jardineria_y_paisajismo.pdf` was indexed with drip specifications, but full retrieval failed (research browser 502; Python TLS certificate verification failure). It does not substantiate report claims about municipal-wide practice. Certificate verification was not disabled.
3. **IFAPA localised-irrigation evaluation, Juan Manuel Bohórquez Caro, 15 October 2014:** [official record](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/agriculturaypesca/ifapa/servifapa/registro-servifapa/9e3f6cfd-009a-4d34-a617-ac23c42a24bb) was indexed, then returned 403 on opening. Useful future lead, not offered as a verified direct download.
4. **2024 woody-crop low-cost LoRa paper:** [USC university bibliographic record](https://investigacion.usc.gal/documentos/67842b4e05ff3328ea836349) identifies Dafonte et al., *Sensors* 24(24), 8104. Full-text access was challenged/rate-limited in this session, so the main report does not rely on its numerical performance.
5. A 2026 smart-tensiometer search lead could not be sufficiently verified from full text; no novelty, cost or performance claim from it is adopted.

## Original calculations and proposed design

The pressure relation and pump-energy expression follow hydrostatic pressure and hydraulic work with water density approximately 1,000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration 9.81 m/s². All example inputs, budget allowances, control requirements, schematic placements and the 48-plant trial in LEARN-016 are author proposals. No source establishes them as site defaults. The final package was checked for local links, arithmetic, figure presence and diagram legibility; no hardware or field trial was performed.
