This is a design recommendation, not a silent deletion or a conclusion that deferred taxa are unsuitable. Four other species would increase interpretation/identification demands in a tiny trial. Treatment dimensions, safe spacing and actual nursery stock are deliberately left for technical confirmation. Pair protocol: match microsite and stock within species, record pair IDs and all baseline differences, randomly assign added stone treatment after pairing, retain existing cover in both treatments, standardise preparation/guards/watering rules within pairs, and log every deviation. Avoid connecting runoff between treatments. No fertiliser, organic-mulch substitution, deep ripping or artificial nurse-canopy treatment is included. Undisturbed reference quadrats indicate background change only and are not equivalent experimental controls. Six pairs cannot demonstrate general species superiority, mature canopy outcomes or long-term drought resilience.

CHAPTER SEVEN. MONITORING AND INTERPRETATION

Author-proposed monitoring dataset: plant ID, pair ID, species/stock batch, assigned treatment, position, initial condition, planting/preparation/mulch specification, date, observer, alive/dead/uncertain state, consistent growth metric, condition, litres supplied, watering reason, maintenance minutes, rainfall observation source, photograph ID and deviations. Missing and uncertain states remain distinct; do not replace them with zero. Replacements get new identities and are excluded from original-plant survival. Record all original outcomes, including failures.

One baseline plus 12 monthly visits gives 156 scheduled plant-date records for 12 original plants. At least 90% completeness means at least 141 of those 156 records, counting valid recorded dead/uncertain states rather than dropping them. Monitoring budgets include baseline in technical work and 12 subsequent visits separately. Extra drought-response visits require a named responsible person and resources; they are not assumed free.

Proposed 600-litre first-year logistics capacity is 50 litres per plant on average; it is neither a biological dose nor guaranteed sufficiency. The EUR 60 allowance is an inherited cash assumption and does not prove that 600 litres can be supplied for EUR 60. Obtain actual supply/delivery costs, source/right and ecologist-set planting/rescue triggers before proceeding. No automatic mulch water saving is assumed. Meter volumes per plant and compare within species/pairs. Without a second water treatment, the trial does not causally estimate different watering regimes.

Proposed management threshold of 10 of 12 original plants alive at 12 months is an explicit author decision target, not an expected 83.3% result, a regional standard or a legal funding criterion. Combine it with no unacceptable habitat damage, transparent water/maintenance records and an agreed technical review. Report paired observations and uncertainty; repeated monthly observations are not independent replicates. Scale only after reviewing evidence, limitations and a suitable larger design.
