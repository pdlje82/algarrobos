These are original, code-drawn geometric explanations, not copied publication figures, botanical depictions or measured finca sections. Rectangles identify the nursery root ball; arrows illustrate dimensions, possible root access or water movement. The drawings do not specify a universal hole size, mature rooting depth, catchment size or watering schedule.

All PNGs are 1400 × 850 pixels, rendered directly from their companion SVG. The SVG files contain accessible titles and descriptions, text and editable geometry, without external assets. Text bounds were checked and the rendered figures were visually inspected.

Evidence mapping is given in the report captions. Figures 1–3 combine general site-preparation and planting principles with explicitly conceptual geometry; Figure 4 explains the runoff-capture mechanism supported by the carob field literature and demonstration reports.
