# Designer species additions — 29 October 2026

Seven additions, each with independent whole-plant and botanical-detail views:
coscoja (Quercus coccifera), encina (Quercus rotundifolia), Phillyrea angustifolia,
Phillyrea latifolia, Myrtus communis, Pistacia terebinthus and Quercus suber.
All fourteen transparent RGBA originals are 1254 x 1254 pixels. Generated with
image_gen, copied unchanged; exact prompts, original paths and SHA-256 checksums
are in designer-illustrations-manifest.json. No existing illustration was replaced.

Key review features: coscoja's rigid spiny leaves and prickly acorn cups; encina's
grey-felted leaf undersides; cork oak's thick fissured bark; opposite narrow versus
broad serrated Phillyrea leaves; myrtle's five petals and numerous stamens;
terebinth's terminal leaflet and unwinged rachis, unlike lentisk. Whole-plant habit
varies with site, age and management. Fruit and foliage details are illustrative,
not a phenological calendar. Images do not validate site suitability.
