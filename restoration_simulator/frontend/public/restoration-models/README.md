# Restoration Model Explorer assets

`rozas-altas.png` is the original embedded photograph from **Figure 5.56, page 141**, of Navarro Cerrillo, R. M., and Palacios Rodríguez, G. (eds.), *Soluciones innovadoras para consolidar el algarrobo como cultivo de alto valor en Andalucía*, UCOPress / Universidad de Córdoba, 2026. DOI: [10.21071/000069](https://doi.org/10.21071/000069).

Credit: **QUERAT (2024)**, as stated in the figure caption. The book's page 6 specifies [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Attribution, noncommercial use and share-alike terms apply to the photographic extract. These terms do not change the application code's licence.

The embedded JPEG 2000 image was decoded to a **631 × 468 PNG** without cropping, retouching, rescaling or upscaling. `manifest.json` records the source PDF, its checksum, figure identity, dimensions and asset checksum. The source PDF remains unchanged. The other finca and mixed-dehesa photographs reuse the already credited files under `public/planting-explorer/carob/`.

The three parcel diagrams are original, code-drawn SVGs in `RestorationModelExplorer.jsx`. They are conceptual teaching views, not surveys, measured canopy/root sizes, hydraulic designs or planting-density prescriptions. Their captions and accessible descriptions preserve that distinction.
